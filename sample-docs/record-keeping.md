# Record Keeping and Data Retention Policy

> Sample document written for this demo. "Harbor Point Exchange" is a fictional company and the figures below are illustrative, not regulatory guidance.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines which records Harbor Point Exchange keeps, how long they are kept, and who can access them.

## 2. Retention periods

### 2.1 Customer records

Customer identification documents and CDD files are kept for 5 years after the business relationship ends or after the last transaction, whichever is later.

### 2.2 Transaction records

Records of every exchange and transfer transaction, including amounts, currencies, rates, fees, sender and beneficiary details, are kept for 5 years from the transaction date.

### 2.3 Compliance records

Alert investigations, STR files and related correspondence are kept for 5 years from the date the case was closed. Records linked to an ongoing investigation or legal case must not be deleted until the Compliance Officer confirms in writing that the case is closed.

## 3. Format and storage

Records may be stored electronically as long as they can be retrieved and printed within 2 working days of a request from the regulator. Paper records received at branches must be scanned within 5 working days and the originals sent to the central archive monthly.

## 4. Access

Access to customer and transaction records is limited to staff who need it for their role. Compliance and Internal Audit have read access to all records. Every access to STR files is logged and the log is reviewed quarterly.

## 5. Deletion

After the retention period ends, records are deleted in a quarterly purge run by IT and approved by the Compliance Officer. A deletion log listing the record types and date ranges removed is kept for a further 2 years.
